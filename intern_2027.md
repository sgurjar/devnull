For economic consulting, your application should demonstrate more than programming proficiency. Firms want someone who can use data to answer economic questions and communicate conclusions.

B. Economics

- Intermediate microeconomics.
- Industrial organization.
- Game theory.
- Labor economics.

C. Communication

- Writing concise research summaries.
